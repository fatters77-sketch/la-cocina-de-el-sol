Chiles Rellenos

A Mexican Family Recipe with a Texas Story

De Nuestra Mesa (From Our Table)

Chiles Rellenos is a fairly common Mexican dish, but my family has made some adjustments throughout the years. I chose this recipe primarily because it has both personal and cultural significance to me. Growing up in a family of seven, meat was not always something we could afford to have except on rare occasions, which made chiles rellenos feel like a special, luxurious meal. The combination of meat, cheese, time and skill involved in preparing this dish has made it into something that I associate with comfort, celebration and family.

Ingredients

  • 6 poblano peppers
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 block cheddar cheese
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • cumin
  • 12 eggs, separated
  • flour
  • manteca (lard) for frying

Instructions

  1. Roast the poblano peppers until the skin is blistered, being careful not to over-roast them.
  2. Place the roasted peppers in a produce bag with a small amount of salt and water. Close the bag and allow the peppers to steam until the skins loosen.
  3. Remove the peppers from the bag and gently scrape away the loosened skin with a spoon.
  4. Carefully make an opening in each pepper and remove the seeds without tearing the pepper. Set the cleaned peppers aside.
  5. Season the ground beef with garlic, salt, black pepper, and cumin and cook until the beef is completely browned. Drain the cooked beef in a colander and set it aside.
  6. Cut the cheddar cheese into small rectangular pieces that will fit inside the poblano peppers.
  7. Set aside a small amount of flour to use for sealing the stuffed peppers.
  8. Hold one poblano pepper in the palm of your hand and carefully fill it with the cooked ground beef and one or two pieces of cheddar cheese.
  9. Dip your finger into the flour and lightly apply it to one side of the opening in the pepper. Press the other side against the flour to help seal the opening. Repeat with the remaining peppers and set them aside.
  10. Separate 12 eggs, placing the egg whites into a large mixing bowl and keeping the yolks separate. Be careful not to get any shells or pieces of egg membrane in the whites.
  11. Beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form. The egg whites should be firm enough that they remain in the bowl when it is carefully turned upside down.
  12. Gently fold the egg yolks into the beaten egg whites without deflating the mixture.
  13. Heat the manteca in a large pan until it is ready for frying.
  14. Lightly coat each stuffed poblano pepper with flour.
  15. Coat each floured pepper completely in the egg mixture.
  16. Carefully place each coated pepper into the hot manteca and fry until the outside is golden brown, turning as needed so that it cooks evenly.
  17. Remove the finished chiles rellenos from the pan and allow the excess manteca to drain.
